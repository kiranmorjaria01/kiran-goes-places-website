Patagonia Travel Guide

Torres del Paine is why people come to this corner of Patagonia: granite towers, turquoise lakes and glaciers packed into one national park near the bottom of the world. The W Trek is how most people see it properly, four days walking hut to hut or camp to camp through French Valley, past Los Cuernos and up to the towers themselves.

Quick Facts

Recommended trip time

4-5 days for the W Trek, plus travel time to and from Puerto Natales

Popular Spots

Torres del Paine (W Trek), Puerto Natales

Best season

November to March, the Southern Hemisphere summer, for full trail access and the longest daylight.

Main Language

Spanish

Currency

Chilean peso (CLP)

Daily budget

$60-80 within Torres del Paine (independent/backpacker travel)

Common Questions

How long does the W Trek take?

Four days is the standard circuit: Las Torres on day one, the long 23km day past Los Cuernos to Camping Frances on day two, the French Valley on day three, and Grey Glacier, including an ice hike, on the final day.

When's the best time to hike the W Trek?

Seasonal flights into Puerto Natales, the gateway town, run roughly October to April, which is effectively the trekking season. Book campsites well ahead if you're going during high season, since they sell out.

Is the weather really that unpredictable on the W Trek?

Yes, genuinely. Torres del Paine is famous for throwing every kind of weather at you within a few hours, the local saying about four seasons in a single day holds up, especially crossing the exposed, notoriously windy ground near Lago Skottsberg.

How do campsite bookings work on the W Trek?

Campsites are split across two separate, rival companies, so you can't book the whole route through a single system. It's worth mapping your entire route before booking a single night, since availability across both companies affects which direction and pace actually work.

Can I do the ice hike on the Grey Glacier as an add on?

Yes, it's worth thinking about booking separately from the core trek. It involves crevasses and an ice cave, and comes after a hike along Lake Grey on the trek's final day.

How do I actually get to the start of the W Trek?

From Puerto Natales, if starting in the east, it's a bus to Laguna Amarga then a private, cash only shuttle to the welcome centre near Hotel Las Torres, where the trail begins. A catamaran is involved on other legs, worth booking online in advance.
