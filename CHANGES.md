## Second round of post-review fixes (2026-08-18)

More feedback from reviewing the branch locally:

- **Nav bug wasn't actually fixed the first time.** The earlier fix waited for the browser's single `load` event before re-correcting the scroll position, but on a slower connection the page keeps reflowing for a while after `load` fires too (fonts, below-the-fold images, iframes), which quietly moves the target again and undoes a one-shot correction, exactly the "needs two or three clicks" symptom you reported a second time. `js/main.js` now keeps re-checking the target's position every 200ms for up to 5 seconds and re-corrects each time it moves, stopping as soon as it holds still twice in a row, or the moment you scroll/touch/type yourself. That fixes both problems at once: rows line up evenly again, and a short fact sits centred in its card rather than pinned to the top with dead space underneath.
