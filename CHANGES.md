## Phase 4: making individual posts extractable (2026-08-18)

Every one of the 50 posts got three additions. Verified programmatically that every diff across all 50 files is pure addition, zero lines removed or changed anywhere in the body copy, headings, or existing structure.

- **'The short version' block**, directly under the H1 on every post (before the existing subheading): 3-5 bullets giving the concrete answer up front, so retrieval systems hit facts before the scene-setting narrative that used to open every post. Every bullet was drafted from the post's own body text (re-read in full per post, not summarised from memory), then cross-checked with an automated pass that pulled every number out of the drafted bullets/tables and confirmed it traces back to the source post. That check flagged 41 apparent mismatches; every one turned out to be a word-form-vs-numeral difference (source says 'twenty' or 'a hundred and twenty pounds', bullet says '20' or '£120'), a fact stated in the post's own title rather than its body (e.g. '48 Hours in Antigua'), or a regex artefact (matching the '60' inside 'V60 coffee'). None were fabrications.
- **Facts tables on 17 of the 50 posts** ('altitudes, day counts, costs, distances, durations' per the brief), reusing the exact `.quick-fact` card component built for the destination hubs in Phase 3, not a new visual pattern. Only added where a post actually has a real cluster of numbers worth surfacing, mostly the trek/cost/altitude-heavy posts (Machu Picchu's ticket cost breakdown, both Salkantay altitude profiles, the W Trek day-by-day stats, Tikal's build stats). The other 33 posts don't get one; forcing a table onto a narrative food-crawl or hostel-review post would be padding, not extraction. **Fixed one real bug while building this**: the reused `.quick-fact` card's paragraph inherited the site's `.article-body p` margin when nested inside a post body, giving each card an ugly 40px gap it doesn't have on the hub pages. Added a scoped override (`.article-facts .quick-fact p { margin: 0; }`) before this ever shipped.
- **Visible 'last updated' line** under the byline on every post ('Updated 18 August 2026', matching the `dateModified` already set on the JSON-LD in Phase 2). Caught and fixed a placement bug during this: the line first landed inside the byline's inner flex wrapper (a `.article-byline > div` nested around the name/date) instead of below the whole byline block, because a non-greedy regex matched the first `</div>` it found rather than the outer one. Fixed by anchoring the insertion point to the cover image that always follows the byline instead.
- **Heading rephrasing was assessed and deliberately not done as a blanket change.** The brief's example was replacing a vague heading like 'The trek' with a specific, question-phrased one like 'How hard is the Salkantay Trek'. I pulled every H2/H3 across all 50 posts (39 have them; 11 are pure narrative with none) before touching anything, and found the site doesn't actually have vague headings to fix: the 13 numbered 'things to know' posts already use full declarative statements as headings ('Altitude is the biggest challenge', 'It's generally harder than the Inca Trail'), the day-by-day itinerary/vlog posts use chronological markers ('Day 1: Humantay Lake and the Sky Camp') that would get strictly worse if converted to generic questions, and the numbered listicle posts use place/activity names that are already the extractable unit (and already power the ItemList JSON-LD from Phase 2, which rewriting would break). Converting these wholesale into interrogative phrasing would be a genuine body rewrite, not the light touch-up Phase 4 asks for, and would fight the site's established voice. If you want a different call on this, say so and I'll revisit specific posts.
- `dateModified` in JSON-LD needed no change this phase, it was already set to today's date in Phase 2 and this session's edits confirm that's accurate.
