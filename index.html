About

Chasing stories worth stopping for, one border at a time.

Kiran is a Manchester travel vlogger who trades the familiar for the unfamiliar: trekking to Everest Base Camp in Nepal, standing beneath the Torres del Paine in Chilean Patagonia, watching sunrise from Guatemala's Acatenango volcano and hiking through Peru's glacial valleys to Machu Picchu. The goal is always the same, take people somewhere they haven't been and make them feel like they're actually there.

He's also a UK broadcaster and stand up comedian, which means the storytelling instinct runs deep: pacing, timing, and knowing what actually makes an audience keep watching. That mix of on the ground adventure and broadcast level polish is what makes the content travel further, on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok alike.

FAQ

Who is Kiran Goes Places?

Kiran Goes Places is the travel brand of Kiran Morjaria, a Manchester travel vlogger and broadcaster. He's one of the UK's travel vloggers making adventure and destination travel content, trekking, backpacking and cultural immersion, built to make people actually want to go there.

Where has Kiran travelled?

Recent trips include trekking to Everest Base Camp in Nepal, Torres del Paine and the Atacama Desert in Chile, Acatenango volcano and Machu Picchu in Guatemala and Peru, plus content from Japan, Sri Lanka, Thailand, Laos, Bolivia, Colombia and the Philippines, among others.

Is Kiran available for brand and destination partnerships?

Yes. Kiran works with tourism boards, hotels, resorts and travel adjacent brands to create content that's entertaining, shareable and genuinely drives bookings. Get in touch via the contact form below.

Does Kiran do hosting and live events?

Yes, including travel expos, brand launches and campaign events. Alongside travel content, Kiran is an NHS doctor with regular live TV experience (BBC Morning Live, BBC Asian Network) and a stand up comedian, so he's well practised at hosting and presenting in front of a camera or a crowd, worldwide.

Where is Kiran based?

Manchester, UK. He films across the UK and internationally for the right trip.
